AI Monitoring & Operations New

Technical & production intelligence for your equipment

A compact, non-invasive device plus a cloud platform that monitor your equipment 24/7 — without accessing patient data. They track the technical health of every scanner (helium level, cold head, magnet pressure, quench risk) and combine it with your center's production data to deliver operational, financial and staffing intelligence across MRI and CT.

  • 24/7 real-time technical monitoring: helium level, cold head, cryocompressor, magnet pressure and quench risk
  • Predictive maintenance: detects anomalies and degradation before they become a costly failure or quench
  • Production control & forecasting: finance, staff efficiency and shift planning
  • Integrated remote support and one dashboard across all your sites, equipment and brands

AI Radiology Reporting

Report in 70% less writing time

A high-performance DICOM viewer built by radiologists, natively integrated with AI voice dictation. The radiologist dictates, the study's measurements are injected automatically and the structured report is ready in a fraction of the time — cutting writing time by 70% and speeding up results delivery to your patients.

  • End-to-end workflow: open the study, dictate by voice and the structured report is generated with measurements already loaded — without switching windows
  • Built by radiologists, for radiologists: smart hanging protocols, 2D · MPR · 3D · VR and 1000+ slice studies loaded in under a second
  • Customizable report templates and Word/PDF export with digital signature
  • Keep your current viewer: the AI dictation can be deployed over your existing platform (subject to evaluation)
  • Privacy by design: local processing and integration with DICOM, PACS/RIS and HIS

AI Image Enhancement

Faster, sharper studies — with lower dose

FDA-cleared deep-learning AI that improves image quality and speeds up studies on your existing equipment — with no hardware changes. It accelerates MRI acquisition by up to 80%, denoises low-count PET studies with any radiotracer, and enables up to 75% patient dose reduction in PET-CT.

  • MRI: up to 80% faster MRI acquisition while preserving diagnostic quality
  • PET: denoises low-count acquisitions with any radiotracer, on existing PET, PET-CT and PET-MR — up to 75% lower patient dose
  • CT: ultra-clear CT and synthetic contrasts with no extra acquisition
  • Runs on your existing scanners — no new hardware — and integrates with your PACS/RIS
