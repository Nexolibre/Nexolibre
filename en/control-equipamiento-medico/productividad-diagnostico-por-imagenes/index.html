KPIs

The management KPIs you see on the panel

All calculated per unit, per modality and per site, and filterable by period.

Exams per hourStudies performed divided by the hours the unit was actually working. The most direct measure of a room's throughput.
Utilization and occupancyWhat share of available hours the unit spent acquiring images. The hour-by-day heat map shows which slots stay empty.
Patient changeover timeThe gap between the end of one study and the start of the next on the same unit: positioning, preparation and dead time. High values flag no-shows or gaps in the schedule.
Duration per protocolMedian and average for each study description, and the list of studies that took far longer than usual so you can review them one by one.
Monthly goal and forecastExams done against the unit's monthly goal, month-end forecast and exams per day needed to get there.
Productivity per shiftExams, average duration and exams per hour for morning, afternoon and night, with the peak hour and work done off-shift.
Punctuality and no-showsFrom the worklist: scheduled vs. seen patients, no-shows, on-time percentage and average delay.
Benefits

What changes when you measure each unit's output

  • See which unit, shift and time slot are running below capacity, with data instead of gut feeling.
  • Adjust the schedule where changeover time or no-shows eat into scanner hours.
  • Know mid-month whether you will hit the goal, while there is still time to correct course.
  • Measure the effect of every change (a new protocol, an extra shift) by comparing two periods.
  • One standard: every site and unit measured with the same KPIs.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which productivity KPIs does the system measure?

Total and daily exams, exams per active hour, working hours, median and average study duration, patient changeover time, utilization per unit, occupancy by time slot, atypical studies, monthly goal attainment and productivity per shift.

Do we need to enter data by hand?

No. KPIs are calculated from the studies the units already send to the PACS. The only things you configure are your own decisions: unit aliases, monthly goals and shift hours.

How does it tell whether I will hit the monthly goal?

It forecasts month-end with three methods: historical pace, weighted recent pace and an adjustment for the planned working capacity left. It shows the status (ahead or behind) and how many exams per day you need.

Can it compare shifts, units or periods?

Yes. You can compare units with each other, morning, afternoon and night shifts, and any two periods side by side, even protocol by protocol.
