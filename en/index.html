Real case

A new client's first coil — repaired and back in service

DISS (Diagnostic Imaging Supplies & Services), from the Dominican Republic, sent us a part for the first time at our US laboratory: a Hitachi 0.3T Joint Coil (MR-RGP-8A) with an imaging fault and cosmetic damage. We repaired it — electronics and cosmetics — instead of buying a new coil, in about 2–3 weeks and kept the client informed at every stage. On Aug 11, 2026 they confirmed the coil working with a patient.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Nexolibre do?

Nexolibre provides multivendor technical support and repair for MRI and CT systems, recovers critical parts in its own laboratories, sells equipment and parts, and offers AI software for diagnostic imaging. It is part of Grupo Nexo.

Do you repair MRI coils and parts given up for lost?

Yes. Our laboratories recover MRI coils, RF and gradient modules, power supplies and injectors that the traditional channel writes off, with both electronic and cosmetic repair.

Which brands do you work with?

We work multivendor with MRI/CT equipment from any manufacturer, including GE, Siemens, Philips, Canon, Hitachi and Toshiba, with no OEM lock-in.

What warranty do your repairs have?

All our repairs include a 90-day warranty. Every part is tested and documented before delivery.

Which countries do you operate in?

We have local teams in Argentina, Chile and the United States, and AI software in Colombia. We are part of Grupo Nexo.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the part and the fault. An MRI coil, for example, is typically repaired in 2 to 3 weeks, with the client informed at every stage: evaluation, quote and delivery.

How do I request a quote or send a part for repair?

Message us on WhatsApp at +54 9 11 6741-0993 or use the contact page with the brand, model and part number. We reply promptly with an evaluation and quote.

Do you have MRI/CT parts in stock?

Yes. We keep a catalog of recovered, tested parts — coils, gradients, RF, power supplies and injectors — searchable by brand, model or part number.
